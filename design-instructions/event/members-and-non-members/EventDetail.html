About the Evening

Join us for an unforgettable evening celebrating the longest day of the year. The Midsummer Solstice Soirée promises an atmosphere of unparalleled elegance, featuring a curated five-course tasting menu by Executive Chef Laurent, paired with rare vintages from our private cellar.

The night will conclude with live orchestral performances and a private viewing of the coastal fireworks display from the Grand Terrace. Dress code is strictly black-tie.
